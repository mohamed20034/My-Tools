# Mohsen Sales OS — Hotel Renewals

A personal browser-based workspace for hotel-only Tripadvisor Business Advantage renewal preparation.

## Tools included
1. **Renewal Call Copilot** — builds a hotel-specific call outline from stated needs, non-sensitive notes and verified account evidence.
2. **Hotel Discovery Builder** — creates open-ended discovery paths for common hotel challenges.
3. **Product Value Comparator** — positions Business Advantage, free listing, Sponsored Placements, Meta/metasearch and Direct Book Max as distinct categories. It is not a definitive product specification.
4. **Smart Follow-up Writer** — drafts professional American-English emails from approved, non-sensitive notes.
5. **Hotel Renewal Tracker** — tracks renewal dates, stage, risk and next actions locally, with CSV/JSON export.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages
1. Open **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/(root)**, then Save.
4. When deployment completes, visit: https://mohamed20034.github.io/My-Tools/

## Privacy and limitations
- Independent personal tool; not affiliated with Tripadvisor.
- The tracker stores records in browser localStorage on the current device/profile. It does not sync across devices.
- Use safe aliases. Do not enter guest personal data, passwords, internal account identifiers, confidential rates/terms or restricted company information.
- No connection to Tripadvisor, Salesforce, company systems, email, call recording or external AI API is implemented.
- Generated guidance is based on static templates and the text you enter; it is not a live AI assistant.

## Product accuracy
Verify current Business Advantage inclusions, eligibility, renewal terms, pricing and all non-BA product details in approved internal documentation. Views, clicks and calls are engagement actions, not automatically bookings, revenue or ROI. Do not promise ranking or booking results.
